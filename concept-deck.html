Industrial Asset Reliability & Integrity Platform

From Engineering Knowledge to Engineering Decision

Concept stageNot an approved specificationNovember 2026
At a glance

A platform that turns plant experience into engineering decisions

The problem

Files

Failure knowledge is scattered across people, reports, maintenance records and investigations. It is hard to search, hard to reuse, and rarely connected to the decision it should have informed.

The idea

Files

One platform that converts industrial knowledge and experience into structured cases, practical engineering tools and decision support, for individual professionals and for industrial companies.

The first step

Proposed

Start narrow: a centrifugal pump troubleshooting simulator backed by a small library of reviewed cases. Prove the value before widening the scope.

How to read this deck. Files marks ideas taken from the project document. Proposed marks my own additions and recommendations, open to your challenge.

The core idea

One closed loop, from evidence to reusable knowledge

The document's direction: knowledge should lead to understanding, analysis, decisions and measurable improvement. Files

Training sites end at understanding. CMMS ends at the work order. This platform continues to the decision and its measured outcome. Proposed

The loop closes when each outcome returns to the library as a new, reviewed case. The platform gets more useful with every decision made on it. Proposed

Founding ideas

The four ideas recovered from the project, and the role each plays

IDEA 1Files

Rotating Equipment Intelligence Lab

Pumps, compressors, turbines, gearboxes, seals and bearings, with an interactive troubleshooting simulator.

Role: the simulator and equipment knowledge. The strongest entry point.

IDEA 2Files

Reliability Engineering & Decision Lab

RCA, FMEA, RCM, Bad Actor, MTBF/MTTR and strategy selection.

Role: the analysis workbench. Its edge is linking analysis to cases and decisions.

IDEA 3Files

Asset Lifecycle & Integrity Platform

Asset register, criticality, RBI, lifecycle cost and obsolescence.

Role: a lighter console in a later phase. Too broad to be the first product.

IDEA 4Files

Industrial Failure Knowledge Hub

A searchable base of failures, root causes and lessons learned.

Role: the data layer that feeds ideas 1 and 2.

Experience principles. Arabic-first interface with fixed English technical terms. Mobile first for technicians, wide screens for analysis. Every result shows its source, assumptions, units and evidence grade. The simulator never reveals the answer before the learner commits to a diagnosis. Proposed

AI guardrails

Files

Searches, summarizes and compares approved content only.

Every output is labelled a draft for human review.

Shows the evidence behind each recommendation.

Never invents readings, standards or case details, and never turns a hypothesis into a confirmed cause.

Scope limit. RBI-lite only: prioritization and education. A full risk-based inspection engine is a safety-critical decision tool that needs compliance and specialist review, so it is out of scope for now. Proposed
Value and commercial model

Different value for individuals and companies, one shared foundation

For individuals

Proposed

Practice diagnostic thinking instead of memorizing answers. Use ready analysis tools. Build a record of professional competence.

For companies

Proposed

Preserve experience before experts leave. Avoid re-investigating failures already understood. Measure team competence. Link analysis to decisions and measured results.

Roadmap Proposed

Prove one thing first, then widen step by step

PHASE 0FoundationFix scope. Pump taxonomy. Case model. Review process. Confidentiality position. No coding.
PHASE 1Individual MVPCentrifugal pump simulator, 8 to 10 reviewed scenarios, anonymized case library, two tools: Pareto/Bad Actor and MTBF/MTTR.
PHASE 2ExpansionFull workbench with RCA and FMEA. Then seals, bearings, compressors.
PHASE 3EnterprisePrivate vault, permissions, audit, decision tracker, competency matrix. Pilot with one company.
PHASE 4Integrity ConsoleCriticality, lifecycle cost, light asset register, IOW, optional integrations.
PHASE 5AssistantAI assistant once the library is large enough to give it value.
Phase 1 success test. Real users complete scenarios and come back. Until that is true, nothing later is justified.

This is a sequence only. No dates, team size or effort estimates have been made.

Risks and mitigations Proposed

The four risks that matter most

Risk
Why it matters
Mitigation
Content quality
One engineering error in a scenario destroys trust.
Expert review before publishing, evidence grades on every cause, versioned content.
Data confidentiality
Companies may refuse to share operating data.
Anonymization, private case vault, private deployment option.
Scope creep
The pull to build everything before proving one thing.
One equipment family, one audience, one MVP with a clear success test.
Individuals-only revenue
May not generate enough income on its own.
Keep the enterprise path in Phase 3 and test it with a single pilot company.
